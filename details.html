Synopsis

After a failed heist, courier Aoi inherits a blade that remembers every life it has taken. Each Monday the city redraws its alleyways, and Aoi must map the new streets before the guild finds her first.

The series mixes close-quarters sword fights with quiet rooftop meals and a slow mystery about who is rewriting the city map.
